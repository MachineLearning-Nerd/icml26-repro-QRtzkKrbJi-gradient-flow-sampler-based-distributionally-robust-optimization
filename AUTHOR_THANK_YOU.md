# Thank you to the paper authors

Thank you to Zusen Xu and Jia-Jie Zhu for making *Gradient Flow Sampler-based
Distributionally Robust Optimization* available with a detailed mathematical
framework, explicit algorithms, and associated implementation/data
provenance. Those materials made it possible to audit structural,
theoretical, functional, and empirical claims separately.

In particular, the complete Figure 6 vectors and the functional formulation
made the evidence more informative than a collection of toy plots. This
repository preserves both supporting evidence and counterevidence, including
the unresolved complexity claim, so that the result remains inspectable.

This is independent work by MachineLearning-Nerd. The scoped verdicts do not
represent author review, endorsement, or agreement with the audit.
